Basis for this list
The six categories below are adapted from the Commission on Higher Education's (CHED) official Discipline Group classification, which CHED itself uses to organize and report on higher education programs nationwide. According to CHED's published enrollment statistics, the largest discipline groups by enrollment include Business Administration and Related, Education and Teacher Training, Engineering and Technology, Information Technology, and Medical and Allied, with additional groups covering Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries; Architecture and Town Planning; and Fine and Applied Arts.
